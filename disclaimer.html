Financial Disclaimer

Educational & Informational Notice · Not Certified Financial Advice

1. Estimates Only — Not Financial Advice

The calculations, figures, graphs, and amortization projections generated by the EMI Calculator web application are provided strictly for educational, demonstrative, and general informational purposes only.

The results produced by this tool do not constitute official banking quotations, certified financial planning, tax guidance, investment recommendations, or legal counsel. Kunal Builds is not a chartered accountant, registered investment advisor, lender, bank, or NBFC.

2. Institutional Variance

While our mathematical model utilizes the standard reducing balance formula recognized across Indian banking institutions, official loan statements issued by commercial banks or NBFCs may vary due to several real-world elements:

  • Compounding Conventions: Monthly-reducing, daily-reducing, or annual rest amortization methods applied by different lenders.
  • Ancillary Fees: One-time loan processing charges, documentation fees, stamp duty, valuation fees, and applicable 18% GST.
  • Floating Rate Adjustments: Periodic adjustments to external benchmark lending rates (EBLR / Repo Rate) that change your EMI or tenure over time.
  • Rounding Off Rules: Lender-specific rounding conventions on individual daily interest calculations.

3. Independent Verification Recommended

Before committing to any mortgage agreement, vehicle loan, or personal financing contract, borrowers should:

  1. Consult with an accredited financial advisor or chartered accountant.
  2. Obtain official, written Loan Sanction Letters and Key Fact Statements directly from the lending institution.
  3. Carefully review all pre-closure conditions, partial prepayment charges, and foreclosure terms applicable to their loan category.

4. Limitation of Liability

By using this calculator, you acknowledge that Kunal Builds and its contributors bear no liability for any financial decisions, loan contracts entered into, monetary discrepancies, or consequences resulting directly or indirectly from the use of this software.
