Important Notice Regarding Estimates, Calculators & Financial Information

1. Estimates Only — Not Financial Advice

The calculations, charts, figures, and amortization schedules generated by the EMI Calculator web application are provided strictly for educational, demonstrative, and general informational purposes only.

Under no circumstances do the results generated by this tool constitute official banking statements, certified financial planning, investment advice, tax counseling, or legal guidance. Kunal Builds is not a licensed financial advisor, registered investment advisor, lender, bank, or credit bureau.

2. Variance with Actual Bank & NBFC Quotations

While our mathematical model utilizes the standard reducing balance formula recognized by banking institutions across India (including the Reserve Bank of India framework), the final figures quoted by commercial banks or NBFCs may vary due to multiple practical factors:

  • Different Compounding Schedules: Daily reducing, monthly reducing, or annual rest compounding methodologies applied by specific institutions.
  • Ancillary Fees & Charges: One-time loan origination/processing fees, administrative documentation charges, inspection fees, and stamp duty.
  • Taxes: Government Goods and Services Tax (GST) applied to non-interest banking service charges.
  • Floating Rate Fluctuations: Benchmark interest rate adjustments (e.g., RBI Repo Rate, EBLR, MCLR) that alter EMI amounts or tenure over time.
  • Rounding Off Differences: Institutional round-off conventions and repayment date alignment rules.

3. Independent Verification Strongly Recommended

Before entering into any legally binding loan agreement, mortgage contract, vehicle financing scheme, or credit obligation, you are strongly advised to:

  1. Consult with a qualified, accredited financial planner or chartered accountant.
  2. Obtain official, signed Loan Sanction Letters and Most Important Terms and Conditions (MITC) documents directly from your chosen banking or financial institution.
  3. Carefully verify all pre-closure, partial prepayment, and foreclosure penalty terms applicable to your specific loan category.
