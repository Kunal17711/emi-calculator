About Kunal Builds

Purpose-Driven Software · Financial Clarity in India

Our Mission

At Kunal Builds, our mission is to create clean, privacy-respecting, and lightning-fast web applications that simplify complex everyday decisions. Our EMI Calculator was conceived to offer Indian borrowers a straightforward, transparent tool for computing repayments without intrusive pop-ups, slow-loading frameworks, or unnecessary data tracking.

Engineering Philosophy

Modern web tools often bundle megabytes of analytical trackers, complex frameworks, and third-party dependencies that slow down devices and drain mobile batteries. We build strictly with clean, vanilla web standards: pure HTML5, CSS3, and native JavaScript.

  • Client-Side Execution: All calculations happen locally inside your browser. No financial data or personal numbers are sent to external servers.
  • Zero Dependencies: No JavaScript frameworks or third-party libraries. This guarantees long-term durability and instant loading speeds.
  • Indian Financial Standard: Calibrated with standard reducing-balance math recognized by the Reserve Bank of India and leading financial institutions.

Open Source Transparency

We believe that financial calculators must be verifiable. The full source code of this tool is openly accessible on GitHub under the permissive MIT License. Anyone can review the mathematical logic, inspect the calculation methods, or run the project locally.
