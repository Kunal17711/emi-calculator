About EMI Calculator

Built by Kunal Builds · Purpose-Built Open Source Financial Utility

Our Mission

At EMI Calculator, our goal is simple: to make financial planning fast, completely transparent, and accessible to every borrower in India. Whether you are buying your first home, purchasing a family vehicle, or exploring personal financing options, understanding your long-term repayment commitments should not require navigating clunky, ad-saturated, slow-loading portals.

Why We Built This Tool

Most online calculators on the internet are weighed down by heavy tracking frameworks, intrusive popups, and bloated JavaScript bundles that drain battery life and slow down mobile devices.

Kunal Builds engineered this EMI Calculator using pure, standards-compliant HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. There are zero external dependencies, zero analytical trackers stealing your financial data, and zero server roundtrips. Every calculation runs entirely in your local browser sandbox within milliseconds.

Core Values

  • Privacy First: Your loan numbers, interest rates, and financial queries never leave your browser. We don't store or transmit your sensitive numbers.
  • Indian Financial Alignment: Native support for the Indian numbering format (Lakhs and Crores) and standard reducing balance formulas used by the RBI, SBI, HDFC, ICICI, and major banks.
  • Open Source Integrity: Our codebase is open source and hosted publicly on GitHub, ensuring verifiable mathematical accuracy and complete design transparency.
