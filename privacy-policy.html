Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 · Full Privacy Disclosure

At EMI Calculator, accessible from https://kunal17711.github.io/emi-calculator/, the privacy of our visitors is of paramount importance. This document details how information is handled, our usage of cookies, and third-party advertising practices.

1. Local Client-Side Calculations

Our financial calculation engine executes entirely in your browser. When you enter a principal sum, interest rate, or loan tenure:

  • Your values remain strictly on your local device.
  • No financial queries or calculation parameters are transmitted to any database or external server.
  • We never request account registrations, credit card numbers, or banking credentials.

2. Web Server Log Files

Standard static web hosts log visitors when they load web pages. This information includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), referring/exit pages, date and time stamps, and approximate general geographic metrics. These logs are used solely to monitor platform health, prevent abusive traffic, and administer the site.

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like most websites, our pages and authorized third-party vendors use cookies to store visitor preferences and track general aggregate site interactions to optimize page delivery and browser compatibility.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Vendors

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve advertisements to visitors based upon their visits to this site and other websites across the Internet.

  • Third-party ad vendors and ad networks, including Google, serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other internet sites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to our pages and other online platforms.

5. Opting Out of Personalized Advertising

Visitors retain full discretion over personalized advertising preferences:

  • Google Ads Settings: You can customize or completely opt out of personalized Google Ads by visiting https://www.google.com/settings/ads.
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: You can opt out of third-party interest-based ad tracking through the AboutAds Choices Tool.
  • Network Advertising Initiative: Review ad network opt-out choices at the NAI Consumer Opt-Out Portal.
  • Browser Cookie Settings: You can adjust your browser preferences to reject cookies or alert you when cookies are set. Consult your specific browser documentation for instructions.

6. Rights Under GDPR and CCPA

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may hold statutory data rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request confirmation of whether any personal information is held.
  • Right to Rectification & Erasure: You may request the modification or deletion of any stored data.
  • Do Not Sell My Data: We do not sell or trade personal data to any third party.

7. Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided information through our site, please contact us immediately for prompt deletion.

8. Inquiries and Privacy Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, contact us directly at kkunaall10@gmail.com.
